A place to start

Legal questions can make an ordinary day feel heavy.

Forms, deadlines, and unfamiliar words can leave anyone unsure of what to do next. NyayaSetu is being built to make the first steps easier to understand, and to help people find the right place for support.

For advocates

A clearer view of your practice.

Keep client matters, conversations, and hearing dates in one working space. Help people understand how to reach you and what to expect next.

  • Case rooms for active mattersKeep case details, messages, hearing dates, and document decisions together.

  • A more organized practiceTrack matters and record billable work by case.

Trust, with honesty

Know what this prototype can—and cannot—do.

Identity and advocates

Demo profiles are simulated and are not verified identities or verified advocates. Live DigiLocker sign-in requires an approved integration and is not represented by a demo login.

Your information

This prototype saves case-room and demo information in this browser. It is not a secure, shared cloud case-management service. Avoid entering real Aadhaar, financial, or confidential case information.

Legal support

NyayaSetu offers legal information, tools, and connections. It does not replace advice from an advocate who has reviewed your situation.

    DigiLocker Partner Portal Registration Guide

    Official Onboarding for Ramdeobaba University (RBU) / IDEA Lab NyayaSetu

    To enable live DigiLocker OAuth 2.0 Login without receiving "Invalid Client" from government servers, your university/department must register as a certified Requester Organization on the official government partner portal.

    Step 1: Access the Official Partner Portal Navigate to the official Government of India API Setu partner registration gateway:
    https://partners.apisetu.gov.in (or partners.digitallocker.gov.in).
    Step 2: Sign In with Institutional Authorized Signatory The registration must be initiated using the personal DigiLocker account of an authorized university authority (e.g., Registrar, Dean R&D, Head of CSE Department, or Director of IDEA Lab) using their Aadhaar-linked mobile.
    Step 3: Register as a "Requester Organization" Under registration category, select Requester Organization (not Issuer). Provide:
    • Organization Name: Shri Ramdeobaba College of Engineering and Management / Ramdeobaba University, Nagpur
    • Department: Department of Computer Science & Engineering / IDEA Lab
    • Official Email: Institutional email domain (e.g. @rknec.edu or @rbunagpur.in)
    • Use Case Description: Citizen legal literacy, e-KYC verified statutory pleading drafting, and privileged lawyer-client case document exchange under Advocates Act 1961.
    Step 4: Verification Call & Digital Agreement Signing The NeGD / DigiLocker Partner Evaluation Committee reviews academic applications. An authorized representative will participate in a brief verification call and electronically sign the standardized Terms of Use / Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).
    Step 5: Obtain API Credentials & Whitelist Redirect URI Once approved, the API Setu dashboard provides:
    • x-client-id (App Client ID)
    • x-client-secret (Confidential Secret)
    • In the portal's App Settings, register the Callback URL: http://localhost:3000/callback.html (or your deployed server domain).
    Step 6: Enter Keys into NyayaSetu Paste your Client ID and Client Secret into the "API Keys & Partner Setup" tab in NyayaSetu. Live real DigiLocker login will immediately activate!
